What does Edge mean?

Edge shows how much better a combo has performed compared to what we would expect based on each player’s individual hit rate.

Formula:
Edge = Actual Combo Rate − Expected Combo Rate

Actual Combo Rate is how often all players in the combo hit the selected prop on the same day.

Expected Combo Rate is calculated by multiplying each player’s individual rate together.

Example: if Player A hits 70% and Player B hits 60%, the expected combo rate is:
70% × 60% = 42%

If that combo actually hit 55% of the time, then:
Edge = 55% − 42% = +13%

A positive edge means the players have hit together more often than expected. A negative edge means they have hit together less often than expected.
